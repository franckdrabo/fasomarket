3. Use of Data

Your data is used for the following purposes:

  • Service provision: Account creation, listing publication, messaging, payments
  • Security: Authentication, fraud prevention, abuse protection
  • Communication: Push notifications about your activities (messages, payments, disputes)
  • Service improvement: Aggregated (anonymized) statistics to improve user experience
  • Legal obligations: Compliance with applicable financial and tax regulations

4. Data Sharing

Your personal data may be shared with the following third parties, only to the extent necessary for service operation:

  • CinetPay (payments) — For Mobile Money payment processing. CinetPay is a PCI-DSS certified payment provider. Learn more: cinetpay.com
  • Africastalking (SMS) — For sending OTP verification codes by SMS. Learn more: africastalking.com
  • Cloudinary (image storage) — For hosting listing and profile photos. Learn more: cloudinary.com
  • Firebase / Google (push notifications) — For sending push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). Learn more: firebase.google.com

We never sell your personal data to third parties. Data sharing is limited exclusively to technical service providers necessary for Application operation.

5. Data Security

Bazario implements strict technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption at rest: Sensitive data (email, refresh tokens) is encrypted with AES-256-GCM in the database
  • Password hashing: Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (salt 12) — they are never stored in plain text
  • OTP hashing: Verification codes are hashed with Argon2id
  • Secure transmission: All API communications use HTTPS/TLS
  • Rate limiting: Protection against brute force attacks on authentication endpoints
  • JWT: Token-based authentication with automatic refresh token rotation
  • Helmet: HTTP security headers (X-Content-Type-Options, CSP, etc.)

7. Your Rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable local legislation, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Obtain a copy of all personal data concerning you
  • Right to rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object: Object to the processing of your data for direct marketing purposes
  • Right to restriction: Request restriction of the processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at: contact@bazario.com

We commit to responding to your request within 30 days.

8. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all your personal data by contacting us at contact@bazario.com. Deletion will be carried out within 30 days, subject to legal retention obligations (transaction history retained for 5 years).

Deleting your account results in:

  • Deletion of your profile and all your listings
  • Deletion of your messages (conversations will be anonymized)
  • Deletion of your push notification tokens
  • Deactivation of your access to the Application

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Bazario mobile application does not use cookies. The only storage technologies used are:

  • SecureStore (iOS) / EncryptedSharedPreferences (Android): Secure storage of authentication tokens on the device
  • AsyncStorage: Local storage of user preferences (non-sensitive)

10. International Data Transfers

Your data is hosted on servers located in France and Europe (via our infrastructure providers). Third-party providers (Firebase, Cloudinary, CinetPay, Africastalking) may transfer certain data to countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA). In such cases, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place (standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions).

11. Protection of Minors

The Application is not intended for persons under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at contact@bazario.com so that we can proceed with its deletion.
